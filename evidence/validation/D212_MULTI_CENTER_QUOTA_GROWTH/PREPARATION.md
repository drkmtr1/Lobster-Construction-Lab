## Exact encoding

Require distinct positive absolute edge differences at the original endpoints. Because there are n+1 vertices and n edges, these conditions fill the full label and weight alphabets. All cuts 1 through n are eligible. Every actual center must choose one cut c and threshold D. At its label a the only possible threshold is c−a when a<c, or a−c+1 when a≥c. The actual crossing differences must be [D,n]. Require all cuts distinct and for each pair compare each host's span membership against smaller threshold membership. There is no alpha condition, root pin, or specified cut order.

At a partial labeling, each cut retains the largest known noncrossing weight (default 0) and the smallest known crossing weight (default n). Any eligible D lies strictly above the first and at or below the second. All old edges with known endpoints retain their weights throughout recursion. The current options for an assigned host therefore contain its options in any completion. Reject when an assigned host has no options, or the union of cut options for assigned hosts has fewer cuts than assigned hosts. Distinct final cuts make both tests necessary. At complete graceful labels every edge is known. Then the tested inequalities require every weight at least D to cross, and every smaller weight not to cross. Thus the same test becomes exact. The weight-n edge ensures a crossing edge at each proper cut. No separate approximation is used in a complete join.

The join assigns one actual host-cut option at a time and keeps options compatible with every already assigned host. It tries all remaining eligible coordinates in the selected host's deterministic order. The distinct cut and threshold checks and the two pairwise span checks implement precisely the coefficient equalities, by Section 3 of the proof. The solver chooses the smallest option domain first; it prunes only empty domains or violated exact restrictions. Its successful assignment is fully named. It may stop at the first audited witness per base. It does not count all named labelings.
