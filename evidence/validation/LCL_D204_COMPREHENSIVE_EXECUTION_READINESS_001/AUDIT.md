## Engineering boundaries

The ordinary controller should own the worker Job directly. A fixed interruption fixture may use an additional bounded observer or owner with a private one-use channel. A dedicated transport thread may block in Windows I/O. The controller must never take an unbounded join, block the watchdog on disk, or accept without measured cleanup and terminal receipts. The tested Windows policy remains sampled, with no guarantee of instantaneous cutoff or worst-case cleanup latency.

Qualification allowances are four revised candidates, sixteen native probes per candidate, forty-eight overall, at most 10 observed CPU seconds and 30 observed elapsed seconds per probe including cleanup. At most 1,000 pure regressions, 7,200 observed aggregate preparation CPU seconds, 14,400 observed task wall seconds and 64,000,000 aggregate new evidence bytes. Actual measurements and gaps in coverage will be distinct. The science policy and ceilings stay as frozen. This audit supplies no scientific disposition.
