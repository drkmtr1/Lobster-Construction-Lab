## Proposed structural question

Select the ordinary cut-spectrum direction. The reason is that it can challenge the source of a proved topology barrier while retaining exact old-incidence accounting and the same requirements of independent supply and continuation. The D143 case is a negative control on the selected outside topology and a positive control on its own admitted families. A new state should record its actual crossing and noncrossing spectra, both exact target complements, and a physical connector whose usable label is known before the next section. Test whether a bounded exceptional band around the crossing threshold can be independently supplied and returned, and require at least a reusable multi-section transition, or a scoped obstruction that isolates why the exception budget fails. The next protocol must show exactly which premise of D153 would change. Moving weights or changing an aggregate count symbolically would leave the named fresh incidence problem to prove.

This is a prospective choice, with no new state theorem or run. If an exact residual question survives symbolic analysis, design one matched hybrid diagnostic on the same named objects with the relocated spectrum versus the strict D143 control. It would require an independently checked original-bound witness or an exact restricted obstruction, finite stopping conditions and separate authorization. D156 has no such unresolved mathematical bottleneck in its authorized profile.
