## Operative mathematical interfaces

### Transfers and mixed branches

**LH-E04/LH-E05/LH-E11.** Let actual donor and recipient labels be `a,b`. If a set of currently attached component roots has labels invariant under `r↦a+b−r` and the recipient is outside all moved components, detach and reattach exactly those roots. Their internal labels and edges stay fixed. For each moved root with label `r`, the new weight `|b−r|` equals the old weight of its reflected partner, including a midpoint when it exists. Thus treehood and the whole spectrum are preserved. This directly verifies the pair/singleton form of the attributed Hrnčiar–Haviar rule. LH-E11 Definition 2.2 and Lemma 2.3 supply the same pair and midpoint statements for components. Its Construction 3.2 also needs a graceful zero-rooted source with odd root degree and an exact alternating extreme neighbor set. Theorems 3.4 and 3.7 apply only to the complete patterns in Tables 3.1–3.3. Their branch types refer to parity of an actual center's terminal count. Branch-type counts along the authors' central path are additional constraints. The even or odd size of a class of branches and the parity of each branch quota are different predicates. [Hrnčiar–Haviar](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0012-365X(00)00233-8), [Superdock §§3.1–3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/1403.1564), [Mishra–Panigrahi, §§2–3](https://www.dml.cz/handle/10338.dmlcz/140703)

**Exact contiguous transfer arithmetic.** From a current donor packet `[L,U]` of size `N`, retaining `ell` leaves and moving the nonempty interval `[L+x,U−y]` gives `x+y=ell` and `x−y=Δ=a+b−L−U`. Thus the unique trims are `x=(ell+Δ)/2` and `y=(ell−Δ)/2`. They are legal iff `|Δ|≤ell<N` and `ell≡Δ (mod 2)`. In a nested sequence whose incoming interval sums to the prior and current host labels, Δ equals the next host label minus the prior one. This derivation checks the parity and minimum-retention statement without depending on the ambiguous index wording in the HTML of Lemma 3.15(2). It characterizes one nested contiguous move, with room for the exact reserved tail. It supplies neither that tail nor a target-wide transfer schedule. [Superdock §§3.2–3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/1403.1564)

**LH-E05 exit and composition.** Definitions 3.7–3.10 retain the alternating host list, its transferable leaf set and a well-behaved nested schedule. The bracketed exit in a nicely attainable word is visited only by the last transfer and can carry each prescribed positive remainder. Lemmas 3.12–3.13 concatenate a nicely attainable prefix with an attainable or nicely attainable continuation on the alternating host sequence. For example, Lemma 3.18 revisits hosts with word `1,2,3,4,1,2,3,4,5` to leave four positive even retentions. Lemma 3.19 uses `1,2,1,4,3,4,5` to allow the middle two even retentions to be zero. Section 4.3 uses a back-and-forth first-level schedule to rearrange the admissible second-level transfer order. The even-caterpillar banana theorem is separate, in §4.4. A backward transfer returns to an earlier host in numerical sequence order when the endpoint sum is legal. It does not reverse a prescribed physical target spine. Theorems 4.1 and 4.5 supply diameter-six center-zero labelings under their respective depth and child-parity hypotheses. In 4.1 all leaves are at depth three and the center and depth-one vertices have odd numbers of children. In 4.5 all internal child counts are odd and leaves are at depths two or three. The additional radial theorems 4.2–4.4 each require their full stated restrictions. [Superdock §§3.3–3.9 and 4](https://arxiv.org/html/1403.1564)

**LH-E20 rooted radial control.** The even-diameter tree is centered at the designated odd-degree root. Leaves are all at depth D/2. In the even case all other internal degrees are even, and in the quasi-even case the penultimate level is excepted. Theorem 2.1 pins the center to the maximum and realizes the prescribed extreme neighbor labels by a sequence of pair/single transfers. This differs from a hypothesis that each internal vertex has an even number of children and from an arbitrary root maximum. [Balbuena et al., definitions and Thm. 2.1](https://bibrepo.uca.es/articuloscientificos/3320617x.pdf)

### Matching reconstruction and shells

**Perfect lift (LH-E07/LH-E08).** Contract the matching of an actual 2t-vertex tree and independently supply a graceful contree labeling `g` on `[0,t−1]`. At a contracted pair labeled `z`, use `2z` and `2t−1−2z` on its actual endpoints. Prescribe which member of one pair is even. Propagate along the contree so each nonmatching edge joins two vertices assigned the same parity. A contree is a tree, so there is no consistency cycle. The pair labels partition the two parities of `[0,2t−1]`. Nonmatching weights are exactly twice the contree's differences. The matched weights `|4z−(2t−1)|` are distinct positive odds. Equality for two different z would require `4(z+z′)=4t−2`, which is impossible. This checks the lift algebraically and explains exactly which pins are possible in this construction. In particular a chosen member of the pair with contree label zero can be assigned zero. [Broersma–Hoede](https://combinatorialpress.com/ars-articles/volume-051-ars-articles/another-equivalent-of-the-graceful-tree-conjecture/), [Krop, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/1402.3994)

**Almost-perfect lift (LH-E06).** For a matching of a `2t+1` vertex tree with actual singleton `u`, require an independently graceful contree on `[0,t]` with `g(u)=t` (equivalently zero before complementing). Give `u` label `2t`, use the same two labels for each of the `t` matched pairs, and propagate from `u` even. The same subtraction yields the evens `2,…,2t`, plus the `t` odds. This verifies the conditional lift. The preprint's Proposition 2.5 asserts an almost-perfect matching leaving any specified endpoint of a longest path. For the tree with edges `ab,bc,cd,be`, the matching `{ab,cd}` leaves `e` and `a-b-c-d` is longest. Deleting `d` leaves both `a` and `e` with sole neighbor `b`, so `d` cannot be the singleton. This falsifies the printed endpoint quantifier. Theorem 1.6 needs an existential choice and a valid pin on the corresponding contracted tree. This intake has not repaired or falsified that weaker step, and the endpoint example precludes an arbitrary rooted import. [Krop, Prop. 2.5 and proof of Thm. 1.6](https://arxiv.org/html/1402.3994)

**Perfect-matching lobster route.** Fix a witnessing spine in a lobster with a perfect matching. Every depth-two leaf must be matched to its own center, so every off-spine vertex with terminal children has just one. Contract those pairs into leaves of the spine. Direct leaves of the spine are matched to their spine neighbor and collapse into that neighbor. Contraction of remaining matching edges shortens the spine. The contree is a caterpillar, so an ordinary caterpillar labeling followed by the perfect lift gives the Morgan existence conclusion without a solved lobster target. An additional prescribed contree endpoint and its orientation need a separate check. The algebraic reconstruction here is an alternative verification of the ordinary conclusion at its exact premise. [Morgan](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1571-0653(04)00095-2), [Krop's contree discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/1402.3994)

**LH-E30 shell supply.** A lobster shell has a longest path for which each adjacent off-path vertex has degree two. For a chosen diameter path of a lobster, retain the path, each genuine off-path center and one actual terminal at that center. Delete direct off-path leaves. The chosen path survives and deletion cannot create a longer path, so it remains longest. The shell proof pairs each off-path center with its retained terminal and matches consecutive vertices along the diameter path. This gives a perfect matching if the path has even vertex count, and leaves a chosen path endpoint unmatched if it has odd vertex count. The contracted matched path with the contracted off-path pairs as leaves is a caterpillar. Theorem 2.11's matching-contraction construction establishes ordinary shell gracefulness. The perfect-shell case supports the lift above directly. The almost-perfect shell has an explicit endpoint matching. The shell source supplies ordinary gracefulness. Usable reduced labels, crossing signatures, and both missing palettes after the actual surplus leaves are restored remain separate obligations. The published relaxed bound has a different objective. [Barrientos–Krop, Def. 2.5, Prop. 2.6, Thm. 2.11](https://arxiv.org/html/1402.0196v3)

### Pins, common resource maps and exact matching

**LH-E29, pinned path.** Lemma 2.3's longest root-to-leaf path of `ell` edges in an `n`-vertex tree uses `0,n−1,1,n−2,…`. The remaining labels are `[floor(ell/2)+1,n−ceil(ell/2)−1]` and remaining differences `[1,n−ell−1]`. Theorem 2.4 proves the pinned property for comb full binary trees. Their unrestricted ordinary gracefulness already follows from caterpillar coverage. Example 2.5 separates the pinned graceful and pinned-alpha variants. Finite coverage through order 23 leaves the unbounded full binary question open. LCL's port and cut must coincide physically before any such pin is useful. [Dumitru–Nacu, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12597v2)

**LH-E29, hub map.** For hub label `1` in alphabet `[0,m]`, let `τ(0)=m` and `τ(x)=x−1` at `x≥2`. A label zero at an ordinary hub leaf would contribute weight one, so the zero-containing leg is essential to Thm. 3.3. Each nontrivial leg consumes differences `τ(S)` for its nonhub label set `S`. Disjoint such sets and actual hub leaves for all unused labels give exact full packing. Theorem 3.4 uses `{q+1,2q+1,…,Lq+1}` and weights `{q,…,Lq}` in steps q. Theorem 3.5 supplies zero-containing templates. These are constructive conditions on one physical hub, with no general map for several spine or terminal parents. [Dumitru–Nacu, Def. 3.2 and Thms. 3.3–3.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12597v2)

**Conditional matching translation.** For a fixed admissible partial labeling, put the unused labels R on one side and unused actual differences D on the other. For each physical host `h` and label `x`, add an edge tagged h to `d=|x−f(h)|` iff the difference and crossing predicate are eligible. Retain parallel edges when different physical hosts give the same pair. A matching using every vertex exactly once and exactly the quota of each host exists iff the fixed-core leaf completion exists. Assign each host's selected labels bijectively to its prescribed named leaves. With two colors one quota determines the other, subject to the exact graph and any parallel-edge reduction. For several hosts the full quota vector matters. Equivalently, the requested coefficient of the permanent of the host-weighted matrix is positive. This counts coordinate-to-host assignments, with within-host leaf bijections separate. Exact Matching algorithms for a proved underlying class and error guarantee address the consumer only. Hall coverage without the quota constraints and ordinary flow with fractional solutions do not give this equivalence. [Exact Matching survey](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2023.29), [structured Exact Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03924), [project R226/R232 sources](SOURCES.md)

**Additional controls.** LH-E16's diameter-four pin counterexample refutes an unconditional center maximum even for ordinary gracefulness. Its separate critical/maximum arguments concern specified central or almost-central vertices and constrain alpha-based joins. LH-E17's Theorem 3 uses the authors' spine and restricted leg types, including the exact forbidden ending patterns in Fig. 1. Its matching results have their own contree and balance requirements. LH-E36 uses edge weights `min(|a−b|,2m+1−|a−b|)` and an expanded cyclic alphabet. Its Corollary 2.2 therefore gives a broader topology result for a different labeling objective. [Hua–Krop–Raridan](https://arxiv.org/html/1412.6984), [Luiz–Campos–Richter](https://www.ic.unicamp.br/~reltech/2017/17-14.pdf), [Barrientos–Minion, Cor. 2.2](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/892672)
