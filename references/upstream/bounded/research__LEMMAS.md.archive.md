# Lemmas

L001 (proved elementary local construction; H001): choose b=q+1, leaves 1,...,q, next spine vertex c=q+2. The vertex labels are distinct and within R. The root-to-center difference is q+1, leaf differences are q,...,1, and root-to-next is q+2. These are distinct and lie in Q. Therefore a branch and one next edge can be assigned for all stated N,q, with residual R=Q={q+3,...,N}. Reflection gives the corresponding upper-boundary case. The new active label c=q+2 lies below the residual interval, but residual differences start at q+3 rather than 1, so the caterpillar invariant is not preserved. No later continuation is guaranteed. Independent proof review and literature comparison remain open.

L002 (proved threshold for L001 residual): let c=q+2, and R'=Q'={q+3,...,N}. Each possible next label x has difference x-(q+2), so it is usable exactly when x>=2q+5. Thus choices are {2q+5,...,N}, and one exists iff N>=2q+5. If N=4,q=1 then R'=Q'={4}, c=3, and the only difference is 1, which is unavailable. This is a fixed construction obstruction, not a no-completion claim for the original tree. Separate read-only review confirmed L001 and identified this threshold; full independent mathematical review of L002 remains open.

L002 independent review (separate read-only reviewer): proof confirmed for the stated construction, including equality and empty-residual boundaries. Reviewer additionally checked all 406 E002 input pairs occur exactly once and the saved source hash matches. Literature status remains unresolved.

L003 (general obstruction, independently reviewed): N>=4, q>=1 and k=N-q-2>=1. Suppose a q-leaf branch plus next root edge left both unused resources [1,k], with active next label c=k+1. Then all consumed vertex labels lie in [k+1,N], so each leaf difference is at most N-(k+1)=q+1. Each must exceed k and cannot equal c=k+1, already consumed by the root-to-next edge. Hence q distinct leaf differences must fit in [k+2,q+1], containing max(0,q-k)<q values: contradiction. This does not apply to q=0 or exclude other residual interfaces or other graceful labelings.

L004 (conditional same-root equality): If R=Q=S and root a=0, choose b in S and a q-element A in S excluding b with {|b-x|:x in A}=A. The root edge consumes difference b; leaf edges consume exactly A, and used labels are b and A, so both residual sets become S minus ({b} union A). For S=[t,N], t>=1, b=2t+q-1<=N and A=[t,t+q-1] satisfy b-x in A, hence provide such a branch. The root remains active. This is not closure for all suffixes; independent review and literature status are open.

L004 independent review confirmed the one-branch construction and C007; q=0 is included and no subsequent closure follows.

L005 (independently reviewed conditional finite composition). Under H005, assign each center b_i and leaves t_i,...,t_i+q_i-1, then next spine vertex c=t_(r+1). For q_i>0, reflection b_i-x permutes that leaf interval; thus branch labels and branch differences both equal {b_i} union [t_i,t_i+q_i-1]. For q_i=0 both consist only of {b_i}. Each set is contained in the disjoint block [t_i,t_(i+1)-1]. The next edge uses c as both label and difference, beyond all prior blocks. All resources lie in [1,N], hence the assignments are legal and remaining label/difference sets are equal. The unused set can contain gaps below c; the new active vertex is c, so no closure at that vertex is asserted. Although this works for arbitrary finite r and sizes under its hypothesis, initial supply for every lobster fails for this particular construction: q=(1,1) plus one next edge has N=5 while c=7. Other constructions and labelings are not excluded. Originality unresolved.

L006 (separately reviewed L005 ordering optimization). Let F_q(t)=2t+q for q>0 and F_0(t)=t+1. For positive x<=y, F_y(F_x(t))=4t+2x+y <= 4t+2y+x=F_x(F_y(t)). A zero then positive q gives 2t+q+2, while positive then zero gives 2t+q+1. Since every later F is increasing, adjacent exchanges cannot increase the final threshold. Sorting positive q ascending and moving zeros last is therefore globally optimal among branch orderings of L005. For sizes (2,1), the thresholds are 9 and 8 after reordering. The branches must be exchangeable in the local profile; leaf vertices remain distinguishable. This does not establish the minimum threshold among all assignments or arbitrary continuation.

L007 (uniform positive sizes, separately reviewed). For integers q>=1, r>=1, z>=0 set s=q+1, N=sr+z+1. Root 0; assign center s*i and q leaves s*(r-i)+j, 1<=j<=q, for i=1..r. Assign next spine label sr+1 and z direct branches sr+2..N. Centers cover multiples of s up to sr; leaves cover all positive nonmultiples below sr. Leaf differences |s(2i-r)-j| are distinct: same-sign equality forces i and j equal; opposite-sign equality would require 2s(i+k-r)=j+l, impossible since 2<=j+l<=2s-2. They therefore cover exactly the nonmultiples below sr. Root edges cover the multiples and labels above sr. Thus every label 0..N and difference 1..N occurs exactly once. This is an isolated cluster plus one next edge, with no remaining suffix budget. Reflection produces the symmetric root-N labeling. Branch roles are retained. Originality unknown.

L008 (one positive branch, separately reviewed). For any q>=1 and z>=0, set N=q+z+2, center q+1, leaves 1..q, next label q+2, direct leaves q+3..N, root 0. Branch differences are q+1 and q,...,1; remaining root edges use q+2..N. Therefore H006 holds for at most one positive-size branch. q=0-only instances are immediate.

L009 (restricted suffix-compatible construction, separately reviewed). Let K>=1, 1<=r<=K+2, z>=K+1. A first spine section has r branches with one leaf each and z direct leaves; after its next spine edge the prescribed suffix is any caterpillar with K edges. Set N=K+1+2r+z. Put x_i=K+1+i and center 2x_i with leaf x_i for i=1..r. The x_i and 2x_i lie in [K+2,N], and are disjoint because r<=K+2; max 2x_i<=N follows from z>=K+1. Assign all other labels in [K+2,N] to the z direct leaves; set next spine label K+1. Each branch consumes labels and differences x_i and 2x_i; each direct edge and next edge consumes its own label as difference. Remaining resources are exactly R=Q=[1,K] with active label K+1. The existing caterpillar endpoint invariant then completes any prescribed K-edge caterpillar suffix. This is a full construction for the stated family, not closure for depth-two suffixes. Conditions are sufficient, not necessary. Novelty and primary literature comparison remain unresolved.

L010 (mixed-size caterpillar-suffix construction; separate mathematical review): Under H009, use root label 0. For branch i assign center b_i and leaves A_i=[t_i,t_i+q_i-1]. Since q_i>=1, b_i exceeds every member of A_i, and the reflection b_i-A_i equals A_i. The sets {b_i} union A_i occupy pairwise disjoint blocks [t_i,b_i] within [K+2,N], by t_(i+1)=b_i+1 and N>=b_r. Branches therefore consume the same distinct labels and differences. Assign each remaining label in [K+2,N] to a direct leaf; their number is z. Assign next spine label K+1. All labels and differences in [K+1,N] are then consumed; R=Q=[1,K], active vertex K+1. The caterpillar endpoint invariant completes the exact prescribed K-edge suffix. For K=1, q=(1,2), z=8, N=15, centers 6 and 15 with leaves 3 and (7,8), direct labels 4,5,9,10,11,12,13,14, and next 2 leave label/difference 1 for the suffix. Bound is sufficient, not necessary. It may be far stronger than L009 and does not cover arbitrary depth-two suffixes. No literature/novelty claim.
