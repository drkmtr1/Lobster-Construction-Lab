## Independent mathematical audit C001–C006 (2026-11-14)

The following supersedes the provisional disposition above for the six *specific examples*. This is a separate paper-and-pencil argument using the saved resource sets and prefixes, not a replay of the original decision engine. It does not certify that the original campaign enumerated its whole declared domain. The full original examples and hypothesis strings are in `../runs/laptop/results/M02-c9aacd934c38a23cdfd62a5f-a001.json` and `../runs/laptop/results/M03-a31fe738f35dbe1de473ed97-a001.json`. Direct permutation replay is in `../runs/research_audit_20261114/counterexample_replay.json`. All findings are fixed-prefix or local-rule refutations, never claims of nongraceful trees.

- **C001 VERIFIED_REFUTATION — every legal branch keeps one edge.** The claim quantifies over every legal one-leaf branch placement at every reachable star prefix with sufficient remaining cardinality, asserting a legal next root edge. With m=3, prefix root 0 and no edges, R=Q={1,2,3}; the legal branch center 1, leaf 3 uses differences 1 and 2. The remaining label is 2 and remaining difference is 3, so the next edge from 0 cannot be assigned. The alternative center 2, leaf 1, next 3 uses differences 2,1,3 and shows the obstruction belongs to the particular choice. This is edge-minimal within the declared q>=1 domain: branch and next edge require at least three edges. It refutes the unrestricted choice rule, not existence of some branch placement.
- **C002 VERIFIED_REFUTATION — individual branch and root supply suffice.** The claim says that a legal one-leaf branch and at least two root-realizable differences imply simultaneous allocation of that branch and a next edge. Let m=4 and prefix edge root 0 to leaf 2, which uses label 2 and difference 2. Thus R=Q={1,3,4}, and root supply is 3. Exactly four legal ordered (center, leaf) pairs exist: (1,4), (3,4), (4,1), (4,3). Their remaining labels are respectively 3,1,3,1, and in each case the corresponding next difference was already consumed by the branch. The four-pair list exhausts the nine possible distinct ordered pairs, proving local impossibility and verifying the branch-feasibility premise. Minimality across all prefixes is not asserted.
- **C003 VERIFIED_REFUTATION — far-center interval greedy rule continues.** For N=3, q=1, root 0, R=Q={1,2,3}, the premise q+2<=N holds. The prescribed center 3 and near-end leaf 1 consume differences 3 and 2, leaving R={2}, Q={1}; root-to-2 needs difference 2. The alternate C001 allocation succeeds. This is minimal N in its q>=1 domain. It refutes this specific greedy placement, not all interval constructions.
- **C004 VERIFIED_REFUTATION — separate feasibility implies joint feasibility.** At m=3 the prefix is the single root labeled 1, R={0,2,3}, Q={1,2,3}. Two direct branches and a next edge require three labels and differences, so cardinality suffices. Each separate request can use label 0 with difference 1. Jointly, however, root differences available from the three labels are 1,1,2, so three distinct differences are impossible. This refutes the quantified local rule; it does not show a tree is nongraceful. Three requested edges require m>=3, making this edge-minimal for this exact request type.
- **C005 VERIFIED_REFUTATION — separate feasibility plus root supply suffices.** Let m=5 with prefix 0--2, R=Q={1,3,4,5}; request one direct branch, one one-leaf branch and a next edge. The four root differences are available, meeting the required r+1=3; each request is separately feasible (direct/next label 1; one-leaf branch 0--1--4). The total demand is four labels and differences. For any legal one-leaf branch center b and leaf l, its leaf difference d=|b-l| lies in R, differs from b, and cannot equal l because no b=2l is possible among these distinct members of R. Thus d is one of the two labels left for the direct branch and next edge; their root differences equal those labels, so a collision is unavoidable. This exhausts all possible branch placements by argument. Minimality across prefixes is unverified.
- **C006 VERIFIED_REFUTATION — separate supplies at two parents imply joint supply.** Fixed P4 middle edge labels 0 and 3 use difference 3; R=Q={1,2}. Each parent can individually attach a leaf. The only distinct assignments to the two leaves produce differences (1,1) or (2,2), so neither is legal. The unrestricted P4 is graceful in path order 0,3,1,2. This refutes the explicit fixed P4 diagnostic and any general implication containing it. It does not refute unrestricted P4 gracefulness.

For C001–C005, the stated prefix has distinct used labels and edge differences and exactly the saved complementary R,Q. C006 has the same property for its fixed middle edge. Each impossibility argument exhausts the finite choices of its particular example; complete independent re-enumeration of all M02/M03 test domains and proof of minimality beyond the explicit lower bounds above remain outstanding.
