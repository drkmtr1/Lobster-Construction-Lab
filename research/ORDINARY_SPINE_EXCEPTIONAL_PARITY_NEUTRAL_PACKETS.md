## 1. Original counts and necessary threshold

$$
2K=F+r+R+5,\qquad m=n+2K,\qquad n\ge2K .
$$

The upper class consists of $u$, the second centers, the direct leaf and the first terminals, for $N_{\rm high}=4+F$. The connected crossing exterior has these same colors on its reduced label sides. The reduced spectrum makes the terminal balance exact. Keep $vu,uw$. In a reference tree all lower slots $[1,A-1]$ attach to $u$, and every upper slot $A+b$ with $b\ge2$ attaches to $w$. These weights fill $[1,A+B]$. At a true selected first terminal in an upper slot, replacing the reference parent by its actual first center of reduced label $j_i\ge1$ decreases the weight by $j_i$. At a selected second terminal in a lower slot, replacing its reference parent by its actual second center of label $A+b_i$ increases the weight by $b_i-1\ge1$. Terminal-enabled implies that at least one count is positive, and the balance then implies $e_1,e_2\ge1$.

For the same-phase D157 placement $s=2K-A-B$, $k=K-A$, and $A\le K\le A+s$. The smallest old crossing weight $d-1$ gains $s$ and must reach $d+K-1$, giving $s\ge K$.
