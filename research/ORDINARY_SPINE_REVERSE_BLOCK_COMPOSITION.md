# D208 direction-independent reverse-block composition

**Proved within the supplied-list premises.** A pendant-entry, boundary-root alpha block with zero at its actual last receiving spine vertex supplies the same receiving graph in reverse order with a pendant boundary entry and an extreme exit. Starting at an independently supplied ordinary graceful component on the other end, D206 consumes any finite list of those reversed blocks on their exact original connectors. This settles the ordinary gracefulness of the original family proposed in D207, and the corresponding D117 quota family. The stronger D207 monotone return question remains unresolved and deferred. ## 1. Reverse-block supplier

Let H be a named tree with k edges and a graceful labeling g onto [0,k], all edges crossing the cut between A and A+1. Its witnessing spine begins with an actual pendant entry v whose sole local neighbor is the first receiving spine vertex u, and ends at a distinct receiving spine vertex w. Supply g(v)=A and g(w)=0, with 0≤A<k. Every branch and terminal belongs to its original parent. The distinct labels at v and w imply A≥1. Thus k≥2 and both original classes are nonempty.

The only pair across the alpha cut at distance one is (A,A+1). Since weight one occurs and the vertex at A is v, it must be the actual edge vu and g(u)=A+1. In particular u is distinct from w. Remove only the local pendant entry and its one edge from this operand. Its surviving tree R has k vertices and the same original receiving spine vertices, whose order may be read from w to u. Keep every edge and branch incidence of R. Add a local pendant entry e at w, with e a name distinct from R, and use the spine e,w,…,u. This is again a tree. Distances of the surviving vertices to the receiving spine were unchanged by deleting v, since v had no branch and each surviving vertex's path to v passed through u. The new spine witnesses the same distance bound. For a later join, the entry symbol e names the actual existing adjoining vertex; Section 2 specifies that identity.

Set B=k−A. Give the survivors and entry the following labels.

| Original local labels | New assignment | Exact images |
|---|---|---|
| g(z)<A | h(z)=g(z)+B+1 | [B+1,k] |
| g(z)>A | h(z)=g(z)−A−1 | [0,B−1] |
| New entry e | h(e)=B | {B} |

The ranges are pairwise disjoint and exhaust [0,k]. In particular h(w)=B+1, h(u)=0. At the cut between B and B+1, the old sides of every surviving edge are exchanged. For an old edge with endpoints labeled x<A and y>A, its distance d=y−x belongs to [2,k]. Its new positive upper-minus-lower difference is (x+B+1)−(y−A−1)=k+2−d. Thus the surviving edges use [2,k] once each, and the actual new pendant edge ew crosses the cut with difference one. The reversed block has entry at the largest lower label B, actual first receiver w at B+1, and exit u at zero. This proves the precise proposed alpha partition and edge transform.

The hypotheses exclude A=0, k=0 and k=1. For the smallest case k=2,A=1, the block is the actual path v,u,w with labels 1,2,0 in that order; the reversed path e,w,u has the same ordered values. When A=1 or A=k−1 one surviving label interval is a singleton, and the displayed formulas still apply. The witness path from v to w has even positive length because both end labels are lower and every edge crosses, so there are at least two receiving spine vertices. There is no restriction on an actual index permutation beyond reading the spine in reverse. The label reflection on the surviving edges is involutive; reversing again swaps A and B and recovers g on the original receiving vertices, with the original entry recovered when it is restored at u.

## 2. Exact named original and finite composition

Take a finite list H₁,…,Hᵣ satisfying Section 1 with sizes k_i, boundaries A_i and named first and last receivers u_i,w_i. Use pairwise disjoint original receiving graphs R_i=H_i minus its local entry. Let T be an independently supplied ordinary graceful n-edge component, disjoint from every R_i, with a witnessing spine ending at its actual adjoining x when read in the construction direction. Require x to carry 0 or n. Its other spine endpoint may have any label. For the singleton T take n=0 and x its only vertex. For lobster composition require the witnessing spines to concatenate at the stated physical endpoints, so every named vertex is within distance two of the combined spine.

In the original forward spine order put R₁,…,Rᵣ,T. Its exact connectors are w_i u_(i+1) for i<r and w_r x when r≥1. If the original forward chain has an initial singleton spine vertex v₀, also include exactly the actual edge v₀u₁. Every R_i retains all its internal edges, including the original receiving-spine edges and every indexed branch attachment and terminal parent. In the forward local views, the entry of H_i for i>1 is the already existing w_(i−1). Its local edge to u_i is the same original connector that will be installed from the reverse side. The forward entry of H₁ may name the actual v₀ when the original has it. The general theorem is for the stated chain, with or without that singleton. An arbitrary extra left component needs a separate composition theorem.

Normalize the supplied f on T to f(x)=0 by complementing at n if necessary. Process blocks in order r,r−1,…,1. In the reversed view of H_i let its local entry be the actual vertex x_i, where x_r=x and x_i=u_(i+1) for i<r. This is the last installed spine vertex and has label zero before the step. The D206 copy of the local entry is that existing vertex, including any old incident edges which are outside the local block. It gets no second label. Conversely its one local incidence in the new operand is x_iw_i, the exact original connector. Install the reversed receiver with D206, with local boundary B_i=k_i−A_i. Its only shared vertex with the accumulated component is x_i. The outgoing u_i receives zero and is available for the next block. No original vertex of the chain is deleted by replacing a local entry in the isolated proof.

At each step, with N old edges, the old labels are translated into [B_i,B_i+N]. The fresh labels occupy [0,B_i−1] and [B_i+N+1,N+k_i]. Old weights stay in [1,N], and all k_i fresh edges including the connector use [N+1,N+k_i]. The original receiving edges contribute k_i−1 of these; an omitted forward local edge is installed as a reverse connector of another block when i>1, or as the final singleton edge when i=1 and the original has v₀. When i=1 and the original has no v₀, that local supplier edge is outside the original chain. The cut at N+B_i+1 puts all old vertices on the lower side and all new incidences across. The union is ordinary graceful, which suffices for the next step. The reported cut is a consequence of this step rather than an input premise on T or a promised return for its former far endpoint. Complementing the final labeling gives the other physical orientation if desired.

After the r steps the chain without v₀ has M=n+Σ_i k_i edges. If the original includes v₀, append it at the actual zero vertex u₁, with label M+1 and the actual edge v₀u₁ of weight M+1. It is a previously uninstalled original vertex, not an auxiliary. This keeps every prior label and weight and restores the exact original initial spine edge. For the empty list the result is T unchanged. If the declared empty-list original includes a singleton attached to x, the same one-edge completion applies at x instead. The counting formula includes one extra edge only when that original vertex and edge are present. The construction requires no condition on the label at the far endpoint of T beyond its own graceful supply.

### Final emission and boundary resources

Write N_i=n+Σ_(j>i) k_j and S_i=Σ_(j<i) B_j. The original labels are emitted directly in their final coordinates.

- Each vertex of T gets f(z)+Σ_j B_j.
- Each original z of R_i with h_i(z)<B_i gets S_i+h_i(z).
- Each original z of R_i with h_i(z)>B_i gets S_i+N_i+h_i(z).
- An actual v₀, when included, gets M+1.

The label at an already installed vertex receives exactly the sum of the later translations; the local entry is omitted from the new birth list. The disjoint vertex sets T and R_i and eventual singleton account for every original vertex exactly once. The displayed formula is the inductive construction with all later translations evaluated in advance. The connector x_iw_i has final endpoint labels S_i+B_i and S_i+N_i+B_i+1, respectively, with weight N_i+1. Every surviving original edge of local forward difference d has final difference N_i+k_i+2−d. Later translations affect both of its endpoints by the same amount. The bands [1,n] and [N_i+1,N_i+k_i] partition [1,M]; the initial singleton's edge contributes only M+1 when present. This is exact arithmetic on the original edges.

For instance, before processing R_i, the already installed right-hand subchain uses the consecutive label interval [Σ_(j≤i)B_j, N_i+Σ_(j≤i)B_j] in the final alphabet and weights [1,N_i]. The pending resources are their exact complements in the target alphabets, and the only original edge joining installed and uninstalled names is the specified x_iw_i. A boundary between individual original receiving spine sections can likewise be read from the emitted names and differences by taking complements of installed vertices and edges; it need not itself form a complete graceful state. Each step consumes one finite supplied receiver with one literal connector and leaves a zero port. There are exactly r such steps and at most one next singleton step. Thus the construction terminates and both inventories are exhausted.
