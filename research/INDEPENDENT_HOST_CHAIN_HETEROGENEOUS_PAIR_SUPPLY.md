## 2. A chain augments one host

Let s=3−k with k in {1,2,4}; thus s∈{2,1,−1}. On coordinates x₀,…,xₜ with successive difference s, a low chain uses LL xᵢ→xᵢ₊₁ and UU xᵢ₊₁→xᵢ at Lk for each link. Close it by LU H3 at xₜ and UL H3 at x₀. The LL rows use every lower input except xₜ and lower weight except x₀, and the UU rows use every upper input except x₀ and upper weight except xₜ. The two crossings fill precisely those holes. All signed equations are s,−s,0,0 respectively.

A high chain uses LU xᵢ₊₁→xᵢ and UL xᵢ→xᵢ₊₁ at Hk. Its missing lower input and weight are both x₀, and the missing upper input and weight are both xₜ. Close by LL L3 at x₀ and UU L3 at xₜ. The signed shifts on the Hk links are −s,s. In each case t=0 means the two closing rows alone, reproducing the original double at that coordinate. There is no link requiring any particular sign of s: orient ascending for s>0 and descending for s<0. Thus all intervals consume each usable coordinate once in each of the four inventories.

For d=|s| reserve d consecutive doubles of the appropriate kind, and insert p consecutive positions immediately after them. The d residue classes modulo d on the resulting length-d+p interval are nonempty since each contains one of its first d coordinates. They form d progressions of step s after orientation. If their lengths are tᵢ+1, then Σtᵢ=p. The chains preserve 2d reservoir rows, add 2p rows at one selected host and change no quota at the other member of its numerical pair. This also proves the zero-insertion case and treats unequal progression lengths at d=2.

## 4. Named physical decomposition and strict comparison

### Exact earlier homogeneous removal criterion

In the equal-within-pair D231 architecture, every odd nonbinary quota (at least 5) must occur an even number of times independently on each side. Define N_L,N_R as half the numbers of nonbinary branches on their respective sides, and N=N_L+N_R. Let c_{σ,1} and c_{σ,3} count binary branches on side σ and let E_σ=⌊c_{σ,1}/2⌋+⌊c_{σ,3}/2⌋. The exact condition for removal in homogeneous-within-pair groups of four to a registered binary remainder is
```text
N_σ ≤ C_σ on each side;
p = 4 ceil(N/4) − N;
Σ_σ min(E_σ,C_σ−N_σ) ≥ p;
h − 4 ceil(N/4) ≥ 10.
```
Necessity follows because the nonbinary branches must all be removed, equally paired on their original sides, and at least p binary padding pairs are required to round to a multiple of four. Any additional multiple of four increases capacity and size requirements. For sufficiency, take those N nonbinary pairs by fixed within-quota name order. Distribute p padding pairs among the two remaining capacities, taking binary pairs of equal quota from name order, and partition all chosen pairs into arbitrary groups of four. Each deletion lowers h by four and final h stays ≥10. Even left and odd right parities persist and intermediate sizes dominate the final minima. At N=0 take zero padding and no removals. This criterion applies exactly to this removal architecture with a D229-domain binary remainder. A violation says nothing about other constructions.

## 5. Capacity analysis and one exact ordered exclusion

Within the declared sparse bank design, a single large identity reservoir cannot buy the crossing doubles, and a single large crossing reservoir cannot buy the identity doubles. For example, A=99, B=3 with a1=3 and five other off-reservoir quotas unit has length available but no crossing double for the two-coordinate L1 bank. Conversely A=9, B=99 with b1=3 and other off-reservoir quotas unit has no identity double for H1. This is the row type needed in the bank, rather than a bound on available raw positions. Within the stated cuts the extra double coordinates 0,6,n−3 cannot simply be used with the same interior cut argument: a cut after 0 moves the pinned holes, a cut after 6 separates the L8 endpoints, and a cut after n−3 is beyond the lower regular weight boundary and separates the L7 endpoints. A completed nontrivial chain retains reservoir rows at its endpoints, generally on different coordinates. Their continued use as a new coordinate-matched double is not implied. These limitations leave other banks, transports and numerical placements open.

There is also an exact obstruction independent of the seven exceptional rows. In the same ordered reflected returning S2 architecture, take low quotas (1,1,A,1), high (1,1,1,1), and odd A≥7. Then n=(A+7)/2≥7. Including all declared replacements and excluding the two returns, the lower weight sum minus lower input sum is −4n+6, by the four lost top regular values, the difference of isolated values and the different pinned holes. The upper extra weights n−2,n−1,n,n+1 sum to 4n−2. Thus the net signed displacement over all residual rows is 4. These alphabets are valid here because the D230 formulas and block exclusions apply at n≥6 with both returns available.

There are four high rows total and #LU−#UL=4, so all are LU. Their net displacement is −2−1+0+1=−2. Low rows must supply +6. The only nonzero low residual displacements have unsigned magnitudes 2,1,4,5, at L1,L2,L7,L8 respectively, with one row each. L4's quota is used by its return.
