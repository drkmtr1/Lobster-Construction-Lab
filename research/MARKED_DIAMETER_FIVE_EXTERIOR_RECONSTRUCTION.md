## 2. Exact criterion on a supplied source

For an ordinary graceful bijection f of S write
```text
x = f(s:1), y = f(s:2), a = f(s:0), b = f(s:3),
c = f(b:0:0), d = f(b:3:0),
u = |x−c|, v = |y−d|.
```
The two different source edges have positive distinct weights u and v. With all labels fixed, the common edges of S and T give precisely [1,m] minus {u,v}. The restored left and right edges contribute |a−c| and |b−d|, respectively. Consequently f is graceful on T if and only if their multiset is {u,v}, each occurring once. The vertex alphabet and every unchanged endpoint pair persist. As u≠v, these are exactly two modes.

- **Individual preservation.** The equations |a−c|=|x−c| and |b−d|=|y−d| give a=x or 2c−x and b=y or 2d−y. Distinct actual vertices have distinct labels, so only a=2c−x and b=2d−y are possible.
