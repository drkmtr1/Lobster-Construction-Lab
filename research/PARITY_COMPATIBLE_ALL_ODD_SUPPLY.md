# D222 — Parity-compatible geometry and independent all-odd supply

**PARTIAL POSITIVE, PROVED WITHIN THE STATED SCOPES.** The old D218–D221 slot bands exclude every registered all-odd input with the prescribed central roots and valid fixed-label restoration. The proposed imbalance-three geometry, marked seed and three independent quota parameters are valid. A different construction supplies every registered multiplicity with at most one nonunit quota, at any chosen named branch. The remaining multi-branch enlargement problem is unresolved. ## 1. Actual original and the parity obstruction

Use the three literal spine edges between s:0,s:1,s:2,s:3 in that order. In T each branch b:i:u is attached to its original s:i and each terminal l:i:u:e to its own branch, with original zero-based indices. The D217 folded tree S replaces only the left and right exterior branch attachments by attachments to s:1 and s:2 respectively. The two graphs use the same named vertices and each branch is attached singly to the spine. Set ℓ+j=2h−3, so h≥5. There are 2h−1 branch centers, F terminals and two spine-end slots in addition to the two inner roots. Thus m=2h+2+F. All quotas odd implies F is odd, m is odd, and m≥4h+1.

For every graceful tree with m edges, the sum of its actual edge differences and the sum of its vertex labels both equal m(m+1)/2. For an edge uv, |f(u)−f(v)|≡f(u)+f(v) modulo 2. Summing by incidence and subtracting the vertex sum gives
```text
Σ_v (deg(v)−1) f(v) ≡ 0 (mod 2).
```

In S give s:1,s:2 labels 0,m. Let a,b denote the labels of s:0,s:3 and c,d those of the two exterior branch centers, in physical order. Let C sum all 2h+1 slot labels and E sum just the labels of the even-quota centers. Both spine ends and every terminal are leaves in S. Each branch center contributes its quota times its label to the parity sum, so together these centers contribute C−a−b−E modulo 2. The right root has j+2 slots and its spine edge to the other root, so its degree minus one is j+2, which is odd. The left root has label zero. The identity on S is consequently m+C−a−b−E≡0.

In [D217 fixed-label restoration](MARKED_DIAMETER_FIVE_EXTERIOR_RECONSTRUCTION.md#2-exact-criterion-on-a-supplied-source) the folded pair is (c,m−d) and the restored pair is (|a−c|,|b−d|). Under individual preservation, a+c≡c and b+d≡m+d, so a≡0 and b≡m. Under exchange, a+c≡m+d and b+d≡c. Adding again gives a+b≡m. Thus E≡C is necessary in both modes, independently of how the terminal assignments were obtained.

For the D218–D221 bands [1,h+1] ∪ [m−h,m−1], the h numerical pairs {k,m−k} and the unpaired h+1 give C=hm+h+1. With all quotas odd, E=0 and C≡1. This rules out those numerical bands with the prescribed extreme central roots and a graceful fold plus weight-preserving restoration, for every registered all-odd input, even if the slots and terminals are allocated jointly. In fact those same prescribed root labels and bands also rule out a direct graceful labeling of T itself. In T the endpoints s:0,s:3 each have degree two and contribute a+b, joining the center contribution C−a−b−E, while s:2 has degree minus one j+1 even and s:1 is zero. The sum on T is C−E, which is C on the all-odd domain. These exclusions leave other label bands, root labels and ordinary supply available.

## 2. Complementary bands with general imbalance

Take integer s≥0 with L=h+1+s, H=h−s≥0 and δ=L−H=2s+1. Use disjoint slot bands [1,L] and [m−H,m−1], with the middle terminal alphabet nonempty. Give each of the H pairs {k,m−k} with 1≤k≤H entirely to one folded root, and all δ unpaired slots H+1 through L to the right root. The left needs (ℓ+2)/2 pairs and the right needs (j+2−δ)/2. These integers sum to H, and nonnegative counts are necessary and sufficient for this side allocation absent additional role pins. In particular the unpaired slots must fit on the right.

Each pair contributes k and m−k, independently of side. The unpaired low slots give [m−L,m−H−1] at root m, and the root edge gives m. Thus the skeleton consumes [1,H] ∪ [m−L,m]. Labels left for terminals are R=[L+1,m−H−1] and differences are D=[H+1,m−L−1]. Both intervals have length m−1−(H+L)=F since H+L=2h+1. All slot labels are outside R. With i=z−(L+1) and w=|z−host|−(H+1), low host k gives w=i+δ−k and high host m−k gives w=F−1−k−i. This uses absolute differences on the actual terminal edge.

The low unpaired range has mean (H+1+L)/2=h+1, so C=Hm+δ(h+1). For odd m, its parity is (h−s)+(h+1)≡1−s. All-odd supply through this geometry with valid restoration therefore requires odd s. This is a necessary skeleton selection rule. It does not ensure any terminal assignment.

## 3. Marked skeleton with imbalance three

Set s=1, L=h+2, H=h−1 and δ=3. Take b=h+1 for even h and b=h+2 for odd h. This is an odd slot in [h,h+2]. Put a=m−1, c=(b−1)/2, d=(b+1)/2. Then c≥3 and c<d≤(h+3)/2≤H for h≥5. The six marks (0,m,a,b,c,d) are distinct. Put pair indices 1 and c on the left and d on the right. The pair at 1 supplies a and a left interior branch, the pair at c supplies c and another left interior branch, and the pair at d supplies d and a right interior branch. The unpaired b is s:3. The other two unpaired slots take right interior branches. The left needs (ℓ+2)/2≥2 pairs and the right needs (j−1)/2≥1, summing to H. Thus there are enough remaining pairs, with every spare pair supplying two interior centers on its assigned side. Assign them to available original indices in increasing numerical pair order, then increasing branch index. This defines a physical host for every original branch without changing quotas.

The folded weights c,m−d exchange upon restoration: m−1−c=m−d and b−d=c. That exchange concerns the two actual exterior center edges. Its arithmetic is independent of the terminal counts. The rest of this proof supplies terminals only on the subdomains stated below.

## 5. An independent construction at every multiplicity with one enlarged quota

This route labels the original directly. For any registered ℓ,j and any chosen named branch center u, first specify the all-unit original T₀ as the subgraph with just terminal index zero at every branch. Put the edge from each branch center to its terminal in a matching, together with the two spine edges s:0--s:1 and s:2--s:3. These pair every original vertex exactly once. Contract the matching only as an aid to calculation. The two contracted spine pairs form adjacent hubs, each attached to its own contracted branch leaves. The hub on the left has ℓ+1 leaves and the one on the right j+1, including the respective flank. The auxiliary double star has n=ℓ+j+3=2h edges.

Write A for the leaf count at the hub containing the selected branch pair, and B for the other leaf count. Here n=A+B+1. Assign that selected hub n, its leaves [0,A−1] starting with the chosen branch pair at 0, the other hub A, and the other hub's leaves [A+1,n−1]. This is an explicit bijection onto [0,n]. Its actual double-star differences are [n−A+1,n] at the first hub's leaves, n−A=B+1 across the hubs, and [1,B] at the other hub's leaves.

Lift each contracted vertex of label g to its two actual matched vertices with labels 2g and M₀−2g, where M₀=2n+1. At the chosen branch pair assign the even label 0 to its center and the odd label M₀ to its terminal. Propagate the assignment of the even and odd endpoints across the contracted tree so that each actual nonmatching edge joins endpoints of the same label parity. Given the orientation at one pair, the actual edge to its neighbor fixes exactly one of the neighbor's endpoints as even or odd. The contracted graph is a tree, so each new pair gets one consistent assignment. This propagation keeps all exact original incidences, including s:1--s:2 as the actual nonmatching spine connector.

The pair labels fill [0,M₀] by their even and odd parts. Every nonmatching edge has difference 2 times its double-star difference, so these edges give [2,2n] in steps of two. Matching differences are |M₀−4g|. They are positive odd, and a collision between distinct g's would imply 4(g+g′)=2M₀, impossible modulo 4. There are n+1 such edges, so they give every odd weight in [1,M₀]. This proves the graceful labeling on T₀ with the selected actual center at zero. To supply any positive odd quota Q there while all others remain one, append Q−1 actual terminals at this center with labels [M₀+1,M₀+Q−1]. Their weights are exactly that interval and every old incidence and label is unchanged. The construction preserves the original's entire spine and emits every actual named terminal. It terminates after one traversal of the contracted double star and one finite interval assignment. All unit quotas correspond to empty enlargement.

Morgan's perfect-matching theorem already covers the all-unit original, and the matching lift uses the established complementary-pair matching mechanism, realized directly on this double star. It does not supply an enlarged original merely by invoking that theorem: with any odd quota ≥3, at least two terminals at that branch remain unmatched in any matching, since they have one common neighbor. Krop's almost-perfect theorem cannot apply to that enlarged original either. The diameter of T is seven, attained between opposite flank terminals. Its folded S has diameter five since all folded vertices are within depth two of one endpoint of the inner edge, with depth two on both sides. Bare diameter-five source gracefulness supplies no required D217 marks. The inspected Mishra–Panigrahi degree premise with all but possibly the last central spine vertex even does not include T: the central spine, obtained by deleting the two end edges at each end of a longest path, has degrees (2,ℓ+2,j+2,2) on T, with the odd degree internal in both orientations. The composition and pairing theorems cited here do not justify arbitrary independent multi-branch enlargement. ## 6. Why the immediate D221 contiguous peel does not generalize

Analyze the imbalance-three initial contiguous interval at low 3. Its index and weight coordinates are [0,N−1] for N=F, and the actual endpoint sum is m+3. One attempted two-host step assigns Q₀ and Q₁ positive odd rows at low 3 and high m−2 and returns a centered contiguous interval anchored at low 4. To keep the original endpoint sum, remove K=(Q₀+Q₁)/2 indices at each end, with N>2K. The returned indices [K,N−K−1] would give weights [K−1,N−K−2]. Thus the outer weights would be [0,K−2] and [N−K−1,N−1].

Index the outer terminal bands by b=0,...,K−1 from the lower and upper extremes respectively. Measure upper weights by decline from N−1. Low 3 gives identity in each lane. High m−2 takes lower b to upper decline b+2 and upper b to lower weight b−2. These lane restrictions are forced for any valid row because N≥2K+1, so a lower index at the high host has w≥N−K−2≥K−1, and an upper index there gives w≤K−3. Low identity rows also cannot cross lanes. Let X be the lower indices sent to high m−2 and Y the upper indices sent there. The lower weight count K−1 gives |X|−|Y|=1. Writing S_K for Σ[0,K−1], the baseline lane sums change from S_K to S_K−(K−1) and S_K+K, respectively. Directly summing the four kinds of row gives
```text
ΣX−ΣY+2|Y| = K−1,
ΣX−ΣY+2|X| = K.
```
The difference would require 2(|X|−|Y|)=1. This rules out the exact centered two-host peel, even with arbitrary assignment inside its outer resource bands.

Trying the immediate four-host analogue with lows 3,4 and highs m−2,m−3 also fails on unit quotas under its centered premises. Remove two indices at each end and anchor the contiguous middle at low 5. Its weights are [0,N−5], so the available four weights all lie in the upper lane, with declines 0..3. Lower indices 0 and 1 can only go to the high hosts: low 3 gives 0,1, and low 4 gives −1,0, unavailable to these rows. With one row at each high, the prospective decline pairs from those lower indices are (2,4) or (3,3), both invalid. These arguments exclude neither asymmetric or noncontiguous remainders nor other host orders, slot bands, or joint allocations. They identify a specific failure of transporting D221's contiguous return, rather than a restriction of all-odd graceful supply.

## 7. Exact remaining domain and proposed discriminator

All constructions preserve actual names. Physical reversal sends s:i to s:(3−i), and the branch and terminal section indices to 3−i with all other indices retained. This preserves actual differences and gives the reflected original domains. In the orientation with ℓ even and j odd, the proved ordinary supply is the union of these domains.

- At all registered multiplicities, at most one actual quota is greater than one, at any branch.
- At h=5, all even-side interior quotas are one, at most one odd-side interior quota exceeds one, and both flanks vary independently over all positive odd integers.

The unclosed complement is at least two enlarged quotas for h≥6. At h=5 it is at least two enlarged quotas with either an enlarged even-side interior quota or at least two enlarged odd-side interior quotas. This is a limit of the present suppliers. Independently labeled ordinary cluster composition does not yet supply the required resources for arbitrary multiple enlarged branches. The positive imbalance-three family leaves this problem despite the verified parity condition.

For Director consideration only, a single bounded hybrid discriminator could ask whether the exact target `[[3],[3,3],[1,1,1,1,1],[1]]` has a graceful D217 folded source on the imbalance-three bands with marks (0,27,26,7,3,4), and their exact fixed-label restoration. It has nine hosts, 15 terminals and 27 edges. The matched control `[[3],[1,1],[1,1,1,3,1],[3]]` has the same size, bands, marks and quota multiset, and is supplied by Section 4. The positions of the enlarged quotas differ. The required pair placements at h=5 force the left pairs 1 and 3, and the right pairs 2 and 4 plus the three unpaired slots. The two left quotas are equal and the right interiors are all unit at the target. Permuting equally sized whole arms inside a physical section preserves every pinned vertex and both graph types, so sorted lifting can avoid duplicating identical target roles. This is an existence reduction.

The full fixed-skeleton domain would use actual absolute endpoint differences, admitting every residual label allocation at its original host quota.
