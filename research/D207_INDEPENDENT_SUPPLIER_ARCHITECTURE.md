# D207 independent supplier architecture and coverage audit

The retained architectures prove several useful forms of independent supply and conditional continuation. They establish no coverage theorem for all finite lobster section words. The closest critical gap is a **joint supplier of compatible states for prescribed original incidences**. The program needs complementary ordinary continuation or a proved reconstruction bridge in addition to the retained alpha-block methods. A wholesale change of graph representation is neither required by the evidence nor shown sufficient. The next proposed test isolates this gap on one exact ordinary receiving block with a proved alpha-boundary obstruction. The universal target is a graceful labeling of every actual finite lobster, including the singleton. Given a witnessing spine, any off-spine component of a tree has exactly one attachment to it, since two attachment edges would create a cycle. Distance at most two then gives precisely the physical direct-leaf and center/terminal roles. This yields an exact arbitrary-length section word with ordered quotas and the genuine consecutive spine edges. Existence of that representation is elementary. The difficult structural duty is to find compatible initial states and a partition or derivation by **admitted label-supplying operations** on every word. Even when an original has multiple witnessing spines, the chosen proof must account for at least one valid spine or explicitly justified different reduction. A known counterexample for a prescribed spine interface excludes that interface. A full nongracefulness claim would have to exclude every labeling of the original graph.

A supplier must start from the original profile, exact local shape or explicitly independent input state. A conditional allocator can give a fully rigorous existential certificate when its antecedent is supplied. A catalog of states known to exist because they were obtained by solving the entire target is circular as universal independent supply. A proof of ordinary gracefulness may use simultaneous relabeling, compatible paired factors or a proved reduction and reconstruction. Immutable emission is useful for exact evidence and composition, but preserving every earlier actual label is an additional construction restriction.

### Exact D206 interface discriminator

In the usual section-word attachment to a prefix, the local block consists of a new receiving word and its already present entry vertex (v), with no other new children at (v). Thus (v) is a pendant vertex of the **local** block. If it is the largest lower label (A) in an alpha block, the unique difference 1 forces its actual neighbor (u) to be the smallest upper label (A+1). Deleting (v) and translating the entire upper class down by one leaves the receiver's label bijection and all remaining differences shortened from ([2,k]) to ([1,k-1]). Every surviving edge still crosses the compressed boundary. This also preserves an extreme exit distinct from (v). Conversely an alpha labeling of the receiver with (u) at the smallest upper label accepts the insertion of a new lower boundary label at (v), translating all upper labels up by one and producing weight 1 on (vu). For a singleton receiver the one-edge attachment is handled directly. This is the leaf-entry specialization of the exact contraction relation. [R244](../references/upstream/mdl/N244_contraction_corrections.md) · [D206](ORDINARY_SPINE_BOUNDARY_ROOT_JOINT_BLOCK.md)

Consequently the exact pendant-entry block (((),(4,3),(2))) cannot be an alpha block of this type. Deleting that actual entry would alpha-label the D104 receiver, while D105 shows that the receiver itself has an ordinary extreme-ended solution. The incompatibility also rules out **every pure translation of a fixed minimum-oriented prefix with (n≥14) edges** for this attachment. Indeed (k=14) fresh edges must use ([n+1,n+14]) if all old labels undergo one translation. The old labels occupy ([A,A+n]) with (v) at (A). The available fresh labels are the two exterior intervals. A block edge entirely below, or entirely above, the old interval, counting (v=A) with the lower block class, has difference at most 14 and would duplicate an old weight. Therefore every block edge crosses the two block sides, and translating the upper fresh interval down by (n) gives exactly the forbidden local alpha labeling. This implication is about that fixed decomposition and old translation. Coordinated gaps, non-order-preserving relabeling, another allowed boundary, or a different decomposition remain independent choices.

For an arbitrary alpha block with a pendant entry as above, its outgoing spine vertex lies in the upper class after an odd number of spine edges and in the lower class after an even number. The extreme-exit condition then fixes **which** extreme, in addition to requiring that extreme to be attainable at the actual vertex. For example, a single positive-terminal receiving section cannot have an extreme exit in this leaf-entry D206 setting. Weight 1 already forces the receiving spine vertex to be A+1. Its required upper extreme would therefore force k=A+1, a singleton upper class, but an actual terminal edge at a center does not meet that sole upper vertex. These are interface restrictions. They do not prohibit ordinary one-section supply through another mechanism. D206 gives a strong **consumer of ordinary seeds**, even when this particular way of supplying its next local block fails.

## Dependency map and critical path

```text
exact named graph, bijections, spine or reduction, resource identities
  ├── independently supplied initial states and local block roles
  ├── joint palette and actual-host allocation with extraction
  └── structural alternatives and exact original reconstruction
           │  develop together against actual compatibility tests
           ▼
  nonempty compatible path of states for every prescribed original
           │  valid starts, applicable transitions, final discharge
           ▼
  coverage theorem + well-founded closure and termination
           ▼
  universal ordinary graceful existence
```

1. **Foundations stay established.** The exact graph grammar and two resource alphabets are enough to formulate the problem. N240 and Bounded Search already provide whole-instance representations. Universal feasibility does not follow from representability, interval notation or finite recursion.
2. **Supply and composition are coupled.** Develop independently labeled modules with a documented outgoing state while proving that the proposed consumer can actually use those resources. A catalogue of graceful components with unusable physical ports accomplishes less than a narrower family supplying a missing interface. A general ruleset for completing already feasible joint palettes leaves the strongest bottleneck intact.
3. **Initialization, decomposition and transitions are coupled.** A useful universal initializer can depend on a valid chosen spine and on which next blocks are possible. One need not require one state on every first section, or require every currently admitted state to extend. The minimal positive requirement is at least one justified compatible decomposition and state path for each original. Conversely, hiding a solved target in an initializer or saying to choose a state with a completion simply assumes the theorem.
4. **Coverage and termination must be proved with the invariant.** For true spine consumption, use the strictly decreasing number of unprocessed original incidences. For same-size moves, paired substitutions, reversals or auxiliary constructions, supply a separate well-founded measure or a terminating selection and exact reconstruction. Finite steps on a supplied list establish termination of the consumer, not the existence of a legal list. The final terminal state must exhaust both alphabets on the original names.
5. **Positivity is the resulting existence conclusion.** A constructive spine induction or a positivity-preserving reduction into independently covered bases can establish it. A counting formulation or corrected named-count comparison may suggest an invariant, but positivity and count monotonicity are different claims.
6. **Review and formalization run throughout.** Require independent challenge of incidence, both palettes, parity/capacity and exact scope at each gate; formal verification of an eventual universal proof is a downstream deliverable, with reusable lemmas eligible earlier. Assess progress by the precise quantified obligations discharged.

**The critical path is compatible ordinary supply across a genuinely missing boundary.** Generalized allocation is already exact on several geometries, making an independent choice of labeled cores, reservations and subsequent ports more consequential than another propagation engine. Terminal-free handling has a universal result **conditional on an admitted cut state**; the unproved step is entering or leaving that state at a prescribed positive run. D206 shows that an unavailable middle state can be bypassed. The pendant-entry obstruction shows that independent alpha supply cannot be the only receiving architecture for a fixed partition. R248/R244 offer possible alternatives only after exact ancestry/reconstruction is supplied. The present architectures collectively supply valuable parts of a proof, but no coverage theorem or proof that all possible combinations of these techniques must fail is established. Keep the named two-palette representation as the common interface and develop complementary ordinary block constructions or exact reconstruction. Change the representation if a specific separability or closure condition is proved to be the persistent obstruction rather than merely moving the existential choice into a larger state.
