# D209 independent two-sided ordinary supplier

**Proved for a parameterized subdomain.** The exact D105 central control permits every positive pair of flank quotas. In the D117 domain, the proved constructions cover every unequal pair, every equal pair of size at least the central edge count, and the additional equal pairs specified in Section 6. The remaining equal-flank question is open. The constraints excluded below apply to particular central labelings or embeddings.

## 1. Original graph and retained arithmetic

Use the exact original `[[p],[q,a],[b],[t]]`. In physical order let the spine be L,x,y,R. Section L has one indexed center of quota p, x has centers of quotas q,a in that actual order, y has one of quota b, and R has one of quota t. Every listed terminal belongs to its actual indexed center. The central tree consists of x,y and their three center/terminal groups. It has n=q+a+b+4 edges, and the final original has m=n+p+t+4 edges. All parameters are exact integers. The D117 domain is q≥5,a≥3,b≥2 and the separate D105 control is (q,a,b)=(4,3,2). No construction below pads or changes any original incidence.

[D105](ORDINARY_43_2_EXTREME_SPINE.md) independently labels the 13-edge center with x=0,y=n. [D117](ORDINARY_QAR_ALL_PARITY_SUPPLIER.md) independently supplies the same actual extreme pair throughout its entire parameter domain. In each case the actual right center is 1. D117 derives a core and opposite-host reflections from disjoint unused label and difference intervals and then grows only the actual right-center quota. These are initial exact certificates with labels [0,n] and differences [1,n]. Their complements are also valid on precisely those names.

For D131, take a complete graceful old tree with N edges and actual last spine vertex at label N−δ. A one-positive-center append of quota h≥δ, h≥1, maps every old label z to N+1−z, installs the new center at 0, and gives the new spine vertex N+2+δ. The new leaves take [N+2,N+h+2] with N+2+δ omitted. The old interval is [1,N+1] and the fresh labels are 0 and [N+2,N+h+2]. The connector goes from 1+δ to N+2+δ and has weight N+1. The zero-centered star uses [N+2,N+h+2] once on its actual edges, keeping old weights [1,N]. This proves the exact labels, sizes, and weights used in both applications below. Reflecting the entire intermediate alphabet by its maximum preserves all label and difference multiplicities, as does reading an actual witnessing path in reverse. ## 2. Unequal flanks and the large equal boundary

Suppose the smaller quota is h and the larger quota is j>h. Orient the supplied opposite-extreme central labeling so that the actual port facing the smaller flank carries n. The remaining port carries 0. Read the witness in the direction that ends at the first port and append the smaller flank by D131 with δ=0. The intermediate edge count is N=n+h+2. The actual untouched port now has label n+1. Reverse the witness and use that port next. Its second deficit is N−(n+1)=h+1, so the larger quota j is admitted. If p<t this builds on x first, and if t<p it builds on y first. The names and original spine order in the emitted certificate are always L,x,y,R. The edge bands are [1,n], [n+1,N], [N+1,m], and each append uses only the complementary fresh label set proved above. This establishes every p≠t with the exact indexed parents.

For p=t=s≥n, choose the first actual port at 0 instead. The first deficit is n, and the other port, initially n, becomes 1. At N=n+s+2 complement through N. The remaining port becomes N−1, so its second deficit is exactly one. Every s≥n+1 requested in the task is covered, and equality s=n is covered by the same argument because D131 admits equality in its quota bound. This proves both physical directions by choosing the first side and normalizing the core accordingly.

For comparison, for an arbitrary supplied central labeling and either choice of first port and polarity, write F,Y for the first and the other physical port labels. A first quota s requires n−F≤s. After that step the other port has label n+1−Y in size n+s+2. The deficit without intermediate complementation is s+1+Y>s. The deficit with complementation is n+1−Y. Thus two sequential D131 steps for equal s are possible in this model exactly when some initial labeling and choice of traversal and polarity have F≥n−s and Y≥n+1−s. For the opposite-extreme supply this gives s≥n. At s=1, under normalization the two ports must be n and n−1 in some order, with n−1 first. This characterizes the two-step model and leaves other simultaneous or interleaving placements free.

## 3. The adjacent-top supplier and complementary ports

Fix one actual left center at label 0 with quota z and the other at 1 with quota u. Fix the actual right center at 2 with quota b. These are numerical role choices between the two original x children, with every terminal still assigned to its actual original parent. Let x=n, y=n−c. For now take c=1 and z=b+1, so n=2b+u+5 with b,u≥1.

For the adjacent-top case, the central spine and the three actual center attachments use {1,n,n−1,n−3}. The exact unused labels and weights are

```text
source labels  X = [3,n−2]
target weights D = [2,n−2] minus {n−3}
```

They differ only by the label n−3 and the weight 2. Make a descending path from n−3 to 2 with b drops of size 2 and u drops of size 1 in any chosen finite order. Its total drop is 2b+u=n−5. Every source except the first and every image except the last is common to X and D. The vertices on the path are distinct by strict descent. Give each source of a unit drop to the actual center labeled 1, and each source of a double drop to the actual right center labeled 2. Their endpoint differences are exactly their respective images. Every other source is assigned to the actual zero center, with weight equal to its label. The path removes its start from the available image set and reaches exactly its endpoint. Its image set and the remaining identity labels therefore partition D. There are |X|−u−b=z sources left for identity assignments at the zero center. Sort the labels assigned at each host and assign them in increasing order to that parent's original terminal indices. The two resource alphabets and all original quotas are exact.

In this geometry the quota relation is necessary as well. If the fixed centers are 0,1,2 under the indicated spine pins, all sources are above these centers, so sum(X)−sum(D) equals u+2b for every possible allocation. Here that difference is n−5, giving z=b+1. The stated path supplies every input satisfying it, and a fixed different slot geometry can have different necessary conditions.

For the exact D105 quotas use z=3,u=4,b=2. The path 10,8,6,5,4,3,2 gives centers in actual order (1,0,2), and terminal groups (3,4,5,6), (7,9,11), (8,10), as prescribed. This is an independent certificate of the same named central tree, alongside the original D105 extreme pair.

### An exact one-sided enlargement of the adjacent-top case

At the same spine pins x=n,y=n−1 and left center labels 0,1, allow the actual right center to carry H≥2 and require its selected terminal labels to exceed H. Let U=n−1−H. The unused sets are X=[2,n−2] minus {H}, and D=[2,n−2] minus {U}, with the two removed coordinates in their respective ranges when the construction applies. The sum drop in this one-sided geometry must equal u+bH. Equating U−H=u+bH gives z+b+3=(b+2)H, or z+1=(b+2)(H−1). Conversely this equation and positive u,b give U−H=u+bH>0, and ensure 2≤H<U≤n−3. The path from U to H uses u drops of 1 and b drops of H in any order. All internal positions are common, and each H-drop starts above H. The other sources use the zero-host identities. This proves exact sufficiency, and the moment proves necessity under the stated one-sided premise. H=2 recovers the requested z=b+1 supplier. This includes every z for which z+1 is a positive multiple of b+2. Placements with right terminal labels below H retain a separate signed-moment question.

### Complementary descending-path supply

The same actual core can also be supplied at c=b+2−z whenever c=2, or when c≥3 and n≥2c+3. Retain positive z,u,b and choose z from the originally prescribed q,a. The labels are x=n,y=n−c at their own spine slots, and the three actual center labels are 0,1,2 as above. The used weights {n,n−1,c,n−c−2} are distinct under the stated conditions. The residuals are

```text
X = [3,n−1] minus {n−c}
D = [1,n−2] minus {c,n−c−2}
```

Here every original terminal has a label at least 3, so its three host options shift by 0, −1, and −2. Their total drop must be u+2b. Subtracting the sums of X and D gives n+c−6, equal to u+2b precisely at c=b+2−z. The following explicit disjoint paths show sufficiency with the additional stated conditions. For every path, assign its unit or double-drop sources to the corresponding indexed physical host, and use identity assignments at all remaining sources.

- **c=2.** Then z=b and n=2b+u+4. The only unmatched source labels are n−1,n−4 and unmatched targets n−2,1. Use one unit drop n−1→n−2. From n−4 reach 3 using u−1 unit and b−1 double drops, whose total is n−7. Finish with 3→1 using one double drop. The intermediate path stays at least 3 until this last step, so it skips the label/weight hole 2 and remains disjoint from the top path. At n−4=3 its short initial part is empty.
- **c≥3.** The three unmatched source labels are n−1,n−c−2,c. The three unmatched targets are n−c,1,2. Their lengths sum to u+2(z−1). Choose the top part to use min(z−1,⌊L₁/2⌋) doubles and enough units to reach L₁. If all doubles are used, the total-length bound makes the number of units at most u. Otherwise zero or one unit suffices. The remaining tokens give the middle length. Complete the middle path with c+1→c−1 and double drops ending at 1 or 2 according to parity. Use a separate path from c by double drops to the other of 1 and 2. These tails cover the two parities of [1,c], with the middle path additionally leaving c+1. The total double drops in the two tails is c−1, giving b=(z−1)+(c−1). The top path is entirely above the middle source, and none of the two tails visits a point of the middle path above c+1. Equality in the bound for L₂ gives an empty initial part of the middle path before its first double drop.

In either case each path is strictly descending, all internal vertices are common to X and D, and all starts and ends are distinct in their respective unmatched sets. At the disjoint union of the paths, deleting starts and adding ends converts exactly the selected source set to its images. The other labels are common and sent to themselves. This exhausts both residuals. There are exactly u drops at the one-labeled host, b at the two-labeled host, and z identity sources at the zero-labeled host. The terminal order within a parent can be increasing as above. These certificates use only the prescribed original graph and a finite deterministic list of drops. Their bound n≥2c+3 serves the selected paths. It is not claimed necessary for other allocations in the fixed geometry.

## 4. Exact limitations of the unit-flank interface

Suppose q,a,b are all even. In the central tree every degree is odd except that of y, which is two. Indeed x has degree three, each center has even quota plus one, and all leaves have degree one. For any graceful core labeling, the sum of its edge differences is congruent modulo two to the sum of each vertex label counted by its degree. The former equals the sum of [0,n]. Hence the even-degree vertex y must be even. Since n=q+a+b+4 is even, the pins x=n,y=n−1 fail for every labeling of this central tree. Reversing these two adjacent-top **values** is also impossible for every positive q,a,b, independently of parity. If y=n,x=n−1, weight n forces the actual right center to carry zero. Weight n−1 would then need the pair (0,n−1) or (1,n). No 0–x incidence exists, and the only neighbors of y are x at n−1 and its center at 0. Thus neither pair is an edge. Global complementation gives the equivalent exclusion of unordered adjacent-bottom ports in the all-even case. With Section 2 this rules out every two-D131 unit-flank construction on an initially graceful all-even core, including alternative core suppliers.

There is also an exact pure-translation restriction for p=t=1 on any supplied central graceful labeling. Let a translation put the n+1 old labels consecutively in [A,A+n]; let the remaining exterior widths be A,B with A+B=6. All six fresh edges must have differences n+1 through n+6. The two internal edges of each fresh flank (a three-vertex path between its outer spine vertex, center and leaf) must go between opposite exterior sides because any same-side difference is at most 5<n in this domain. Thus its outer vertex and leaf lie opposite its center. Index lower exterior labels by i=1,…,A with label A−i, and upper labels by j=1,…,B with label A+n+j. A fresh internal lower-upper difference, after subtracting n, is i+j. A connector from old label A+f at a lower endpoint i has offset i−(n−f); with an upper endpoint j it has offset j−f.

If the two centers occupy opposite sides, then A=B=3. With center indices h,k on their own lower and upper sides, the two internal star bands in offset coordinates are [h+1,h+3] and [k+1,k+3], with the common value h+k removed from each. To avoid a repeated offset, the intervals may overlap only at that removed coordinate. Thus |h−k|≥2 and {h,k}={1,3}. The consumed offsets are 2,3,5,6. The required connector offset 4 exceeds either available index, an impossibility.

If both centers are high, there are four low sources i=1,…,4 and two upper center indices 1,2. Each center has two actual low neighbors. Assigning index 2 to a low source i and index 1 to i+1 would repeat i+2. Thus increasing i gives center indices 1,1,2,2, and again the internal offsets are 2,3,5,6. The connector offset 4 forces its source i=4 and its old port at label n in core coordinates. The other connector is at i=1 or 2 and has offset 1. The two old ports are distinct, so that connector needs i=2 and its old port at n−1. If both centers are low, complement the whole arrangement to reduce to the high-center case on a complemented core. Therefore a pure old translation for the unit flanks requires adjacent-top ports in some core polarity. Conversely the two-D131 construction for c=1 has core map n+2−f, whose global complement translates f; so such a core is sufficient to obtain an interval placement up to complementation. In particular the all-even unit case cannot be supplied merely by changing the graceful core and preserving it by a common translation or reflection. This does not exclude interleaving fresh labels with old ones or a direct simultaneous labeling of the original.

## 6. Exact proved domain and a held preparation recommendation

For D105 every p,t≥1 is proved. For D117 the union of the proved suppliers covers:

1. All p≠t.
2. All p=t=s≥n.
3. All p=t=s when some actual left quota z has z+1 divisible by b+2.
4. All p=t=s for which a choice z∈{q,a} has c=b+2−z with 2≤c≤s and either c=2 or n≥2c+3.
5. All p=t=s with b≥min(q,a) and s≥min(q,a)+2.
6. All p=t=s≥2 when some actual left quota z equals 2b+2 and the other left quota u is at least two.

The complement of this proved union in the declared family is solely D117 equal flanks, with 1≤s<n, none of items 3, 4, or 6, and either b<min(q,a) or s<min(q,a)+2. The supplied opposite-extreme two-step D131 model fails throughout this complement. For its all-even unit subfamily, the stronger arbitrary-core two-step and pure-translation obstructions also hold. Ordinary existence with other placements remains open on the complement.

**One held recommendation, subject to separate authorization.** Prepare a targeted simultaneous skeleton-and-terminal allocation on the exact 22-edge original `[[1],[6,4],[2],[1]]`. Its original nine-vertex skeleton consists of its four spine vertices and five actual centers, with all other named vertices left as terminals at their specified parents. The falsifiable question is whether one can supply a distinct-label skeleton with distinct actual skeleton weights and allocate the exact unused labels and differences simultaneously to these named terminal quotas. Permit every lawful label order and port, and record complement symmetry only if proved and explicitly admitted. A complete assignment decides existence for that one graph. A negative conclusion would require exclusion of the full registered domain rather than exhaustion of a favorite translated core. This is a prospective bounded preparation, with proof of any resource/host pruning and an independent original-incidence review before considering numerical execution.

Use as matched symbolic controls the same-size exact original `[[1],[5,4],[3],[1]]`, supplied by c=1 with z=4 and b=3, and `[[1],[6,4],[2],[2]]`, supplied by the unequal-flank argument on the same all-even core quotas. Keep the all-even pure-translation rejection of the target as an adverse interface check, separate from the broader joint-allocation question. The intended generalization is an independent change of core label/weight reservations or an interleaved two-flank allocation for the all-even unit family and then other missing equal quotas. Additional D131 port tuning or pure translations cannot address this unit benchmark. The task opens no successor and authorizes no executable verification.
